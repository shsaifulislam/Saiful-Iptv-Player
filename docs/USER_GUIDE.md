# User Guide: Saiful IPTV Player

**Welcome to Saiful IPTV Player** — A Netflix-inspired entertainment platform engineered for TV, Desktop, Tablet, and Mobile.

---

## 1. Getting Started

### Launching the Application
Upon opening Saiful IPTV Player, you are presented with the **Home Dashboard**, showcasing your continue-watching tray, active IPTV playlists, featured licensed films, and recently played channels.

### Account Registration & Login
1. Navigate to the **Profile** icon in the top-right corner (or sidebar).
2. Enter your Email and Password to create an account or sign in.
3. Enabling **Cloud Sync** keeps your favorites, playlists, and watch progress in sync across all your devices.

---

## 2. Managing IPTV Playlists

### Importing an M3U / M3U8 Playlist
1. Go to **Playlists** (`/playlists`) > Click **Add Playlist**.
2. Choose your input method:
   - **File Import**: Browse for a local `.m3u` or `.m3u8` file stored on your device.
   - **Remote URL**: Paste a valid HTTPS playlist link.
3. Tap **Preview** to inspect the detected channel count and category summary before saving.
4. Tap **Save & Ingest**. The player processes channels in the background without freezing.

### Filtering Channels by Category
- In the **Live TV** section, use the category chips at the top to filter streams (e.g. *Bangla, Sports, News, Movies, Kids, Cartoons*).
- Use the instant search bar to find channels by name or ID.

---

## 3. Watching Live TV & Using the EPG Guide

### Player Navigation & Switching Channels
- **Next / Previous Channel**: Use the on-screen arrows, channel switcher drawer, or the Up/Down keys on your keyboard / remote.
- **EPG Program Guide**: Tap the EPG icon to open the timeline schedule. View the currently airing broadcast, start/end times, and upcoming shows.
- **Favorites**: Tap the Heart icon on any channel card to pin it to your Favorites bar.

---

## 4. Universal Media Player Controls

### Gestures & Playback Controls
- **Double-Tap**: Double-tap on the right side to jump forward 10 seconds; double-tap left to rewind 10 seconds.
- **Vertical Swipe**: Swipe up/down on the right side of the screen to adjust volume; swipe on the left side to adjust brightness.
- **A-B Loop Repeat**: Tap the `[A-B]` button during playback to mark Loop Start (A), tap again to mark Loop End (B). The player will automatically loop the selected segment continuously.
- **Subtitles & Audio Tracks**: Tap the Audio/Subtitle settings icon to switch audio languages, select external `.srt` or `.vtt` subtitles, or adjust subtitle delay timing.

---

## 5. Movies & VOD Catalog & Web Portals
- Browse licensed films by genre (*Sci-Fi, Action, Animation, Documentary*).
- Tap on any movie card to view synopsis, duration, age rating, and high-resolution backdrop art.
- Tap **Resume Playback** to continue watching where you left off.
- For episodic series, browse seasons and select specific episodes directly.
- **Movie Web Portals & Download Hub**:
  - Access pre-configured portals: **MovieLink BD** (`https://4vyu23.movielinkbd.at/`) and **HDHub4u** (`https://new12.hdhub4u.cl/?utm=mn1`).
  - View details, copy portal links, or tap **+ Add Portal** to save custom movie hubs.
  - In **Offline Downloads** (`/downloads`), tap **+ Download URL** to paste direct media download links from these portals and watch offline with full player controls.

---

## 6. Official YouTube Hub
- Paste any YouTube link (full URL, shortened `youtu.be`, or embed URL).
- The app automatically extracts the video ID and loads the video using the official YouTube embedded player in full compliance with YouTube terms.
- Bookmark your favorite YouTube educational or entertainment streams for quick access.

---

## 7. Multi-View Mode (Quad Stream)
- Navigate to **Multi-View** (`/multiview`).
- Toggle between **2-Stream Split View** and **4-Stream Quad View**.
- Assign different IPTV channels to each quadrant.
- Tap any quadrant to transfer exclusive audio focus to that stream while the other streams continue playing smoothly.

---

## 8. Parental Controls & Security Settings
- Set a **4-Digit Master PIN** under Settings > Parental Controls.
- Lock channels or movies above specific age thresholds ($PG, PG\text{-}13, R$).
- Locked content requires entering your PIN before playback starts.
